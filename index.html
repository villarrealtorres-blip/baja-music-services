ABOUT

Composer first. Producer by nature.

Edgar Villarreal is a composer, arranger, pianist and audio producer based in Baja California, Mexico. His work combines musical storytelling with hands-on production and post-production experience.

Much of his professional work has been created under confidentiality agreements, so not every project can be publicly credited. The work shown here represents the style, range and production approach he brings to new projects.
